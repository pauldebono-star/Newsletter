1. EU Accession & Negotiation Process

Montenegro's accession talks continue toward a 2028 target. As of July 2026 the country has closed 18 of 33 negotiating chapters (most recently Chapters 8 – Competition Policy, and 29 – Customs Union), with the remaining chapters at 90-100% technical readiness. Chapters 23 and 24 (judiciary, fundamental rights, justice and home affairs) remain the hardest, requiring structural reform rather than administrative compliance.
July 2026 | EEAS

2. National Legislation – Asylum & International Protection

Montenegro's asylum framework remains the 2018-amended Law on International and Temporary Protection of Foreigners (two-stage procedure: intention declaration, then formal application within 15 days). The Ministry of Interior has moved to extend Temporary Protection for Ukrainian arrivals through March 2026, pending further extension.
2026 | UNHCR Montenegro

3. Migration & Asylum Trends – Border Management & Statistics

Frontex recorded 2,829 detections on the Western Balkan route in 2026, down 19% year-on-year, with Syrian, Turkish and Afghan nationals most common and the Croatia-Bosnia border the main exit point. IOM's Q2 2026 flow monitoring surveyed 1,935 migrants across Albania, Bosnia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia and Kosovo, confirming the declining trend.
