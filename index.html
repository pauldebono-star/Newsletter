Montenegro & International Protection

1. EU Accession & Negotiation Process

The EU is preparing an accelerated plan with Montenegro: closing all remaining negotiating chapters by end-2026 and preparing the first draft Accession Treaty by end of September 2026. Eighteen of 33 chapters are provisionally closed (most recently Chapters 8 – Competition Policy, and 29 – Customs Union, in July). PM Spajić says technical work on the rest should wrap "in a month or two," targeting EU membership in 2028.
24 August 2026 | European Western Balkans

2. National Legislation – Asylum & International Protection

Montenegro's amended Law on Foreigners (in force since early 2026) introduced a more structured, digitalized framework aligned with EU standards. Separately, Temporary Protection for Ukrainian arrivals — due to expire 11 March 2025 — was automatically extended for beneficiaries until 4 March 2027 under a Council of Ministers decision.
2026 | Overview: Law on Foreigners amendments

3. Migration & Asylum Trends – Border Management & Statistics

A Regional Steering Committee meeting in Budva, Montenegro brought together the European Commission, all six Western Balkan partners, and Frontex, EUAA, IOM and UNHCR to agree new migration and asylum cooperation priorities through 2030 (returns, international protection, crisis preparedness). Separately, transit through the Western Balkans has fallen sharply: just over 35,000 migrants in 2025, down from 192,000 in 2022.
2026 | Frontex

4. Government & Political Updates

Montenegro has launched a turnkey electronic border surveillance system for the Ministry of Interior, giving 24/7 monitoring of the "blue border" along Lake Skadar. Work began 10 March 2026 and is due to complete within 15 months, part of Chapter 24 (Justice, Freedom and Security) commitments; the EU has also granted an additional €3.9 million for integrated border management.
